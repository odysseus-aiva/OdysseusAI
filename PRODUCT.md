# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** Developer / builder who is integrating or operating a LiveKit-powered voice AI product. They configure agents, assign tools, tune system prompts, and review call data to iterate on their voice experience. They are comfortable with technical surfaces and expect the platform to feel as capable as the work they're doing with it.

**Context:** Used in a focused work session — a desk environment, usually dim, often late-stage prototyping or production monitoring. They want speed and precision; they do not want to be charmed, they want to be equipped.

## Product Purpose

Synaptic is a full-stack voice AI platform. It gives developers a single environment to build, deploy, configure, monitor, and iterate on voice agents — without stitching together separate tools for LiveKit, agent logic, call history, and analytics. Success means a developer can go from "I have a LiveKit token" to "I have a production-grade voice agent with tools, call history, and real-time monitoring" inside one product.

Near-term surface roadmap: Voice (live), Agents (live), Call History, Call Details, Live Calls, Dashboard, Analytics, Tools, Settings.

## Positioning

The purpose-built studio for LiveKit-native voice agents — the only platform where the orb, the config, the history, and the data are one coherent product.

## Brand Personality

**Bold · Immersive · Futuristic.** The platform should feel like operating serious infrastructure, not filling out a form. The orb is the emotional core — a living signal that the AI is present and capable. Every other surface exists to support that central relationship. The aesthetic should feel like it belongs to the year you're building toward, not the year everyone else shipped.

## Anti-references

**Generic SaaS (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk):** Blue-and-white rounded cards, corporate warmth, stock photography, friendly-but-empty microcopy. Synaptic is a builder's tool, not a customer support product. Nothing should read as "enterprise software that a salesperson demoed."

**Dark AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude web):** Consumer chat UI — minimal floating input, text-only, no structure beyond the thread. Synaptic is a platform with multiple surfaces, not a conversation interface. Anything that evokes "AI chatbot" erodes the platform identity.

## Design Principles

1. **The orb sets the register.** Every surface takes its emotional temperature from the voice experience. Dashboards, call history, and config pages should feel like extensions of the same living system — not separate apps bolted on.
2. **Density earns trust.** Developer tools that look sparse look incomplete. Information should be present and organized, not hidden behind progressive disclosure that assumes the user needs protecting.
3. **Motion is a material, not decoration.** Every transition should communicate something: state change, navigation direction, data loading, agent activity. If motion can be removed without changing meaning, it should be.
4. **Dark is load-bearing.** The near-black background is not aesthetic — it is the canvas the orb needs to glow against. All future surfaces share this foundation; no surface should compete with the light source.
5. **Precision over polish.** A pixel-perfect alignment matters more than a trendy visual treatment. When in doubt, choose clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**Target: WCAG 2.1 AAA.** Body text must hit ≥7:1 contrast against background. Focus indicators must be clearly visible. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Reduced motion must be fully respected — the orb and all animated elements have non-animated fallbacks. No time-limited interactions. Color is never the sole carrier of meaning.
